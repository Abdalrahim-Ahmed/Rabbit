Our Story

From a small idea to Egypt's fastest grocery platform

It all started in 2022 when a group of friends kept running into the same problem — getting fresh groceries quickly and affordably was harder than it should be. So we built Rabbit: a simple, fast, and reliable way to get your everyday essentials without the hassle.

Today, Rabbit operates across 25+ cities, partners with 100+ local suppliers, and delivers thousands of orders every day. We believe that good food should be accessible to everyone, not just those who have time to shop.
