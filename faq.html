Orders & Delivery

Everything you need to know about placing and receiving orders.

Rabbit delivers in 20–40 minutes from the moment you confirm your order. Our dark stores are located throughout Cairo and Alexandria, ensuring near-instant dispatch. If your order is delayed beyond 45 minutes, you'll automatically receive a discount on your next order.

Yes! Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and in-app notification. You can see your rider's real-time location on a live map directly in the Rabbit app. Estimated arrival time is updated every 30 seconds.

You can cancel your order within 2 minutes of placing it through the app (before picking begins). Go to Orders → Your Order → Cancel Order. After picking has started, cancellations are reviewed case by case. Contact live chat for urgent cancellations.

You'll receive push notifications at each stage: Order Confirmed, Picking in Progress, Order Dispatched, and Arrived. SMS notifications are sent as backup. You can also check the live order status in the app at any time.

If an item goes out of stock after you've ordered, we'll notify you immediately and remove it from your order. You'll be refunded for that item automatically within 24 hours. You can also set a substitution preference in the app to receive a similar product instead.

We accept a wide range of payment options: Credit & Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), Cash on Delivery, Vodafone Cash, Orange Money, Etisalat Cash, Fawry, and Rabbit Wallet credits. Apple Pay and Google Pay are available on the mobile app.

There is no minimum order value — you can order as little or as much as you like. However, orders under 200 EGP will incur a small delivery fee of 15 EGP. Orders over 200 EGP qualify for free delivery.

Yes! Delivery is completely free on all orders over 200 EGP. For orders below this threshold, a flat 15 EGP delivery fee applies. We also run periodic free delivery promotions — check the Deals page or enable notifications to stay updated.

Address changes can only be made within the first 2 minutes after placing an order, before picking has begun. To request a change, contact our live support chat immediately. Once picking starts, the address is locked for logistical reasons. Always double-check your address before confirming!

You can reach us 24/7 through multiple channels: Live Chat in the app (fastest response), Email at hello@rabbit.eg (reply within 2 hours), or our Help Center for self-service answers. For urgent delivery issues, always use live chat for the fastest resolution.
